What this app does

The My Apps launcher is a small web page inside Microsoft Teams. It shows one button. When you select it, Microsoft My Apps opens in your web browser.

What information it uses

  • It does not ask for, collect, or store personal information.
  • It does not sign you in and does not use cookies.
  • It does not call any APIs or read your Microsoft 365 data.

Hosting

The page is hosted on Microsoft Azure. Like any website, the hosting service can keep standard technical logs, such as the time a page was requested.

My Apps

After My Apps opens, Microsoft's own sign-in and your organization's policies apply. This launcher has no access to that session.
